## 🔹 Overview · 概述

This is the **online version** of the Singing Bowl installation. It runs entirely in a web browser and requires an internet connection to load the hand‑tracking library and audio engine from CDN. No installation is needed – just open the URL and start interacting.

这是唱歌碗装置的**在线版本**。它完全在网页浏览器中运行，需要联网以从 CDN 加载手部追踪库和音频引擎。无需安装——只需打开链接即可开始互动。

| **Cables · 线缆**    | HDMI cable (computer to TV), power cables for all devices.                                                                                  |

## 📐 Space & Positioning · 空间与定位

| Dimension · 尺寸                 | Recommended · 建议                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Screen height**                | Center of the screen at **eye level** for a standing adult (about 140–160 cm from the floor).                                                             |
| **Distance from screen to user** | **1.5 to 2.5 meters (5–8 feet)**. This allows the user to view the entire oversized bowl while keeping their hand close enough for accurate tracking.     |
| **User's arm reach**             | The user should extend their arm comfortably toward the screen. Their hand should be about **50–80 cm from the camera lens**.                             |
| **Camera height**                | Mount the camera at the **top center of the TV**, pointing downward at **15–20°**.                                                                        |
| **Camera field of view**         | 70–80° is ideal. At 2 meters, this captures roughly 2.5 meters wide – enough to cover the user's hand movements across the bowl's diameter.               |
| **Lighting · 光线**              | Even, soft lighting on the user's hand and face. Avoid strong backlight (windows behind the user) or harsh overhead lights that cast shadows on the hand. |
| **Background · 背景**            | A plain wall or curtain behind the user helps the tracking system focus on the hand. Avoid cluttered or reflective backgrounds.                           |
| **Floor marker · 地面标记**      | Place a subtle marker (tape or small rug) on the floor at the ideal distance (e.g., 1.8 meters) to guide the user.                                        |

## 🔧 Step‑by‑Step Setup · 分步安装

1. **Mount the TV** on the wall in **landscape (16:9)** orientation at eye level.  
   **将电视以横屏（16:9）方式安装在墙壁上**，高度与视线平齐。

2. **Place the computer** nearby – on a small shelf, table, or hidden behind the TV.  
   **将电脑放置在附近** – 小架子、桌子或电视后面。

3. **Connect the computer to the TV** using an HDMI cable.  
   **使用 HDMI 线将电脑连接到电视**。

4. **Mount the webcam** on top of the TV (use a clip, small tripod, or adhesive mount). Point it **slightly downward** toward where the user will stand.  
   **将摄像头安装在电视顶部**（使用夹子、小型三脚架或粘贴式支架）。**略微向下倾斜**，指向用户站立的位置。

5. **Connect the webcam** to the computer (use a USB extension cable if needed).  
   **将摄像头连接到电脑**（如需，可使用 USB 延长线）。

6. **Connect the speakers** to the computer. Position them left and right of the TV, or use a soundbar mounted below.  
   **将扬声器连接到电脑**。放置在电视左右两侧，或使用安装在电视下方的条形音箱。

7. **Connect power** to all devices.  
   **接通所有设备的电源**。

8. **Turn on the TV** and select the correct HDMI input.  
   **打开电视**，选择正确的 HDMI 输入源。

9. **Boot the computer** and open a web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox).  
   **启动电脑**，打开网页浏览器（Chrome、Edge 或 Firefox）。

10. **Navigate to the URL**:  
    **访问以下网址**：  
    `https://greggelong.github.io/singBowl/`

11. **Allow camera access** when prompted by the browser.  
    **允许浏览器访问摄像头**。

12. **Click anywhere on the page** to start audio.  
    **点击页面任意位置** 启动音频。

13. **Test the interaction** – stand at the floor marker, extend your hand, and move your finger along the bowl's rim. The sound should respond instantly.  
    **测试交互** – 站在地面标记处，伸出手，沿着碗边移动手指。声音应即时响应。

## ✋ How to Interact · 如何互动

1. **Stand at the floor marker** (if provided) or at the recommended distance (1.5–2.5 meters from the screen).  
   **站在地面标记处**（如有）或建议距离（离屏幕 1.5–2.5 米）。

2. **Extend your arm** toward the screen, keeping your index finger visible to the camera.  
   **伸出手臂**朝向屏幕，保持食指对摄像头可见。

3. **Move your finger along the virtual bowl's rim** – the sound changes instantly.  
   **沿着虚拟碗边移动手指** – 声音会即时变化。
   - Left/right → changes pitch.  
     左右移动 → 改变音高。
   - Up/down → changes brightness (tone color).  
     上下移动 → 改变明亮度（音色）。
   - Fast movement → adds accents and harmonics.  
     快速移动 → 增加重音和泛音。

4. **Keep your other hand** behind your back or at your side to avoid confusing the tracker.  
   **将另一只手**放在背后或身体侧面，避免干扰追踪。

5. **The small window in the corner** shows your mirrored camera feed.  
   **角落的小窗口**显示摄像头画面（镜像）。

## 🔧 Camera Placement Details · 摄像头放置细节

For the best tracking, follow these camera adjustments:

| Adjustment · 调整                   | Recommendation · 建议                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tilt angle · 倾斜角度**           | 15–20° downward from horizontal. This captures the hand while keeping the background minimal.                                                                             |
| **Distance from hand · 到手部距离** | The user's hand should be **50–80 cm** from the camera lens when extended. At this distance, the hand fills about 20–30% of the camera frame.                             |
| **Zoom / FOV**                      | If your webcam has zoom, set it so the hand is clearly visible. A 70–80° FOV is ideal. If the hand appears too small, move the user closer. If too large, move them back. |
| **Focus**                           | Set to autofocus if available. If the hand is blurry, manual focus may be needed.                                                                                         |

## ❓ FAQ · 常见问题

**Q: What if multiple hands appear in the camera?**  
A: The system tracks only **one hand** at a time (the most prominent one). To avoid switching, we recommend only one person interacts at a time. The user should keep their other hand out of frame. A floor marker guides the user to the ideal position.

**问：如果摄像头中出现多只手怎么办？**  
答：系统每次只追踪**一只手**（最突出的那只）。为避免切换，建议每次只有一人交互，用户将另一只手放在画面外。地面标记可以帮助用户站到理想位置。

---

**Q: What if the hand tracking is jittery or inaccurate?**  
A: Check the lighting – make sure the hand is well‑lit and there are no shadows. Move closer or farther from the camera within the 50–80 cm range. A plain background also helps.

**问：如果手部追踪抖动或不准确怎么办？**  
答：检查光线 – 确保手部光线充足且无阴影。在 50–80 厘米范围内调整距离。纯色背景也有助于提高准确性。

---

**Q: No sound – what should I do?**  
A: Click anywhere on the page. Also check that your system volume is turned up and the speakers are connected and powered.

**问：没有声音怎么办？**  
答：点击页面任意位置。同时检查系统音量和扬声器连接。

---

**Q: The page doesn't load or shows a blank screen.**  
A: Check your internet connection – the page loads libraries from CDN. Also try a different browser (Chrome or Edge recommended).

**问：页面无法加载或显示空白屏幕。**  
答：检查网络连接 – 页面需要从 CDN 加载库。同时尝试更换浏览器（推荐 Chrome 或 Edge）。

---

**Q: Can I use this with a projector instead of a TV?**  
A: Yes – mount the projector above, projecting onto a white wall or screen. Follow the same camera placement guide.

**问：我可以使用投影仪代替电视吗？**  
答：可以 – 将投影仪安装在上方，投射到白墙或屏幕上。遵循相同的摄像头放置指南。

This project was created with deep respect for the craftsmen of Jingdezhen – past and present – who have preserved and refined the art of porcelain for generations.
